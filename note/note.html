DEFINITION

Congruency

Two figures or objects are congruent if they have the same shape and size regardless of their position and arrangement.

Enlargement

Enlargement is a transformation in which all the points of an object move from a fixed point with a constant ratio.

Translation

A translation is a geometric transformation that slides a figure from one position to another without rotating, flipping, or changing its size.

Tesselation

A tessellation is a pattern of recurring shapes that fills a plane without leaving empty spaces or overlapping.

COMBINED TRANSFORMATION

An object can perform more than one transformation and will produce an image based on the transformations involved. In general, the combination between the transformation A and transformation B can be written as transformation AB or transformation BA in the order of the desired transformation. Combined transformation AB means transformation B followed by transformation A
